# DeskSync support policy

This sample policy applies only to the fictional DeskSync demonstration product.

## Support scope
The support team assists with installation, documented sign-in behavior, connectivity and workspace sync. Organization account unlocks and network allow-list changes must be performed by the customer's organization administrator. Desktop hardware failures and unrelated software are outside DeskSync support scope.

## Response targets
Standard support aims to acknowledge requests within two business days. This is an acknowledgement target, not a resolution guarantee. Priority severity is assigned after support reviews business impact. The agent can draft a ticket but cannot assign a confirmed service-level commitment.

## Billing and cancellations
Customers can request cancellation through their organization's subscription administrator. Refund requests must be reviewed by the billing team against the signed customer agreement. This policy does not provide a universal refund period or automatic refund eligibility. The assistant must not promise a refund or quote an undocumented price.

## Escalation
If documented troubleshooting does not resolve an issue, prepare a ticket containing product version, operating system, exact symptoms, error codes, impact, attempted steps and relevant diagnostic evidence. Do not claim the ticket was submitted unless an actual submission system confirms it. Never include authentication secrets or unredacted personal content.
